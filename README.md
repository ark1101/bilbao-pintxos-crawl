# Bilbao pintxos crawl

A quality-first Friday-night loop from **Mazarredo Zumarkalea 22** through the Ensanche and Casco Viejo, then back to the Ensanche. Six bars, about **3.9 km / 50-55 minutes of walking total** before time at the bars.

**Start:** Mazarredo Zumarkalea 22, Abando, 48009 Bilbao  
**Suggested start:** 19:30, when Irrintzi's evening pintxos come out.  
**Pace:** one signature pintxo and one drink per stop; order hot items from the kitchen rather than taking only what has been sitting on the counter.  
**Walking route:** [Open the full crawl in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Mazarredo+Zumarkalea%2C+22%2C+Abando%2C+48009+Bilbao%2C+Bizkaia%2C+Spain&destination_place_id=ChIJS3CTittPTg0RV769WLtIjFs&origin=Mazarredo+Zumarkalea%2C+22%2C+Abando%2C+48009+Bilbao%2C+Bizkaia%2C+Spain&origin_place_id=ChIJS3CTittPTg0RV769WLtIjFs&travelmode=walking&waypoints=Caf%C3%A9+Iru%C3%B1a+Bilbao%7CGure+Toki+Bilbao%7CBacaicoa+Bilbao%7CIrrintzi+Bilbao%7CEl+Globo+Diputaci%C3%B3n+Bilbao%7CLa+Vi%C3%B1a+del+Ensanche+Bilbao)

## Route

### 1. Café Iruña
**Colón de Larreátegui 13** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+Iru%C3%B1a&query_place_id=ChIJCXzrG9BPTg0RuIls92C7DmM) · Friday 09:00-24:00  
**Can't miss:** the made-to-order *pincho moruno* from the charcoal grill; the *serranito* with Ibérico bacon and grilled ham.  
**Why:** a 1903 Bilbao institution with a spectacular Moorish interior, and a stronger reason to stop than a generic counter spread. About 9 minutes from the hotel.

### 2. Gure Toki
**Plaza Nueva 12** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gure+Toki&query_place_id=ChIJ2SYPMslPTg0RUhs46RaMHV0) · Friday 09:00-23:30  
**Can't miss:** oxtail croquette; grilled foie with apple and Pedro Ximénez; scallop with potato and breadcrumbs; cod with pil-pil.  
**Why:** the creative heavyweight of Plaza Nueva, mixing modern technique with Basque recipes. About 10 minutes from Café Iruña.

### 3. Bacaicoa
**Plaza Miguel de Unamuno 2** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bacaicoa+taberna&query_place_id=ChIJQ6ms48tPTg0RED6Sk7NEniQ) · Friday until 22:30  
**Can't miss:** *champiñón a la gloria*; spicy *chorizo al infierno*; *setas del purgatorio* (griddled oyster mushrooms). All are cooked to order.  
**Why:** a small family-run, old-school counter that brings the route back from modern pintxos to direct, hot-off-the-griddle flavors. About 3 minutes from Gure Toki.

### 4. Irrintzi
**Santa María 8** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Irrintzi&query_place_id=ChIJGWPCVFFOTg0RzC3zuFETv4c) · Friday 09:30-24:00; evening pintxos from 19:30  
**Can't miss:** crispy apple with duck and peanut sauce; falafel with shiitake; then choose one of the freshly set-out evening pintxos from the rotating counter. The first two are the source-backed signatures; the counter changes.  
**Why:** imaginative, internationally inflected pintxos and one of the Old Town's livelier serious-food stops. About 6 minutes from Bacaicoa.

### 5. El Globo
**Diputación 8** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=El+Globo&query_place_id=ChIJeQ9ks9BPTg0RhwwmKtrweps) · Friday 11:00-23:00  
**Can't miss:** award-winning gratinated *txangurro* (spider crab); grilled foie; cod gratin; baby squid in its own ink.  
**Why:** one of Bilbao's benchmark pintxos bars; this original branch is the right stop as the route returns to the Ensanche. About 15 minutes across the river from Irrintzi.

### 6. La Viña del Ensanche
**Diputación 10** · [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Restaurante+Vi%C3%B1a+del+Ensanche&query_place_id=ChIJ01w-NddPTg0RQq8SKT1irWI) · official Friday bar hours until 23:00-23:30  
**Can't miss:** *Josellini* (Ibérico coppa, cheese and melting foie on glass bread); Jamón Joselito with tomato and fresh chapata; Jamón Joselito croquettes; mushroom-stuffed peppers.  
**Why:** a 1927 classic, strongest on exceptional Ibérico products and wine. It is literally next door to El Globo and leaves an easy 10-minute walk back to the hotel.

**End:** Walk 0.8 km back to Mazarredo 22. The final two bars are in the Ensanche, so the crawl deliberately does not strand you in the Old Town.

## Current-hours caveat

Hours were checked Friday, 18 September 2026 against first-party pages and current Maps place data. Google Maps marked all stops open during research except La Viña, while La Viña's own site listed Friday service until 23:00 or 23:30 and dinner service from 20:00. If it still looks closed when you reach Diputación, finish at El Globo next door.
